The product

One loop from standard to resolution

Headquarters defines what good looks like. Outlets prove it with photos. Plumb closes the loop.

Standards & checklists

Build inspection checklists from templates or scratch. Set weights, severities and the exact photo that proves each point.

Guided photo submission

Outlet managers get a step-by-step capture flow on their phone. Blurry or off-angle photos are rejected on the spot.

AI image analysis

Computer vision checks branding, display arrangements, cleanliness and uniform compliance against your own reference images.

Automatic corrective tasks

Every violation becomes a task with an owner, a severity-based deadline and clear instructions. Escalates when overdue.

Fix verification

Managers upload an after-photo; the AI confirms the fix against the standard before the task is closed. No more "trust me".

Outlet benchmarking

Rank outlets, compare regions, and spot the repeated violations that point to a training or process problem.

How it works

From checklist to closed task in four steps

Set the standard

HQ publishes a checklist with reference images for what "right" looks like at each outlet.

Outlets submit photos

Managers capture 4 to 6 guided photos in about 15 minutes, weekly or on a surprise request.

AI flags violations

Findings come back in seconds with bounding boxes, confidence and the standard each one breaks.

Fix, verify, learn

Tasks are tracked to closure, re-verified by AI, and fed into performance and repeat-violation reports.

FAQ

Questions we get asked a lot

How accurate is the AI image analysis?

Plumb is tuned on your own reference photos, so it learns what "correct" looks like for your brand. Every finding carries a confidence score; low-confidence items are routed to a human reviewer instead of being auto-flagged, and reviewers' decisions improve your model over time.

Can outlet managers cheat by submitting old or staged photos?

Photos are captured in-app with timestamp and location checks, and Plumb detects duplicates and re-used images. Random "spot-check" requests ask for a fresh photo within 30 minutes.

What do outlet teams need to get started?

A smartphone. Managers sign in with their phone number, follow the guided capture flow, and get notified of tasks on WhatsApp or push. No hardware, cameras or installation.

How does pricing work?

A flat monthly fee per active outlet, with volume tiers. Users, standards, photo analyses and tasks are unlimited. Outlets added mid-month are billed pro rata.

Can we use our existing checklists and brand guidelines?

Yes. Upload your current checklists and brand guideline PDFs and our onboarding team will convert them into AI-checkable standards, usually within three working days.

Is our data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in India (Mumbai region), and never used to train models for other customers. SSO, audit logs and role-based access are included on Growth and above.
